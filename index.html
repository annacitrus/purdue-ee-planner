MAKE IT YOURS

A little planning goes a long way.

Drag any course to another semester, or between cards to reorder it. On a touch screen or with a keyboard, open a course and use “Move to semester.”

Hover or focus a course to highlight its prerequisite chain, direct prerequisites, concurrent courses, and subsequent courses. Select a course to see its description. Amber warnings flag scheduling conflicts, but let you keep exploring.

Use Add semester to add a term to an academic year, ordered Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer. Open a course to save a note about an intended elective or selective, or delete it from your plan. Undo can restore deleted courses. Your plan saves automatically in this browser. Undo reverses your latest changes, and Export downloads your plan as a CSV.
